Write `work/live-review.json` with `status` (exactly one of `pass`, `warnings`, `blocked`), `findings` (array of objects with `severity`, `file`, `description`, `fix`), `limitations` (array), `unverified` (array), `pageCoverage`, `visualCoverage`, and `sourceCoverage`.
